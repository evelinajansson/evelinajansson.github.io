About

I am an economist conducting research in regulatory and energy economics, with experience in both quantitative methods and economic theory. My current research focuses on household behavior and the design of incentives that encourage more efficient use of the electricity grid.

My broader research interests include behavioral economics, environmental economics, and resource economics.

Projects

Efficient electricity use and demand flexibility in local electricity grid markets
View →
The main purpose of the project is to investigate and develop pricing mechanisms and other tools to promote efficient electricity utilization in the local electricity grid in Umeå, as well as to learn from consumer behavior and how this can enable a sustainable transformation of the electricity market and society.
